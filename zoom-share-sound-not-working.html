Unstuck / video calls

Screen share works, but nobody can hear the video's audio

Applies to Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, on Windows and Mac.

By default, screen sharing only sends your screen's picture, not its sound — video and system audio travel through separate pipes, and most apps hide the audio toggle in a place people don't look. This isn't a bug you need to troubleshoot; it's a setting you need to find.

Turn on audio sharing before you share

  1. Zoom. In the "Share Screen" window, before clicking Share, tick Share sound in the bottom-left corner. If you're already sharing, stop, tick the box, and share again — it can't be turned on mid-share.
  2. Microsoft Teams. When you open the share picker, use the toggle labeled Include system audio near the top. It only appears when sharing a screen or window, not when sharing a single browser tab in some Teams versions.
  3. Google Meet. Choose A Chrome Tab specifically (not "Your entire screen" or "A window") in the share picker, then tick Share tab audio at the bottom. Meet only supports system audio through tab sharing.

If the toggle is there but still silent

  1. Check your system output device. If your computer's audio is set to headphones or a Bluetooth speaker, some apps only capture audio from the default system output — switch playback to your main speakers/headphones and confirm the video's audio bar is actually moving.
  2. On Mac specifically, install a virtual audio driver. macOS doesn't let apps capture system audio natively the way Windows does. Zoom and Teams on Mac need a small audio routing tool (Zoom prompts you to install one automatically the first time you tick "Share sound") — if it was skipped, redo step 1 and accept the install prompt.
most common fix

Nine times out of ten, it's simply that the audio toggle wasn't ticked before sharing started — not a driver or permissions issue. Stop the share, tick the box, share again.
