Your phone charges very slowly, and it started suddenly

Applies to iPhone and Android, with the same cable and charger that used to work fine.

When charging speed drops suddenly rather than gradually over months, it's rarely the battery itself — batteries degrade slowly. A sudden change points to something blocking the connection or a background process quietly draining power as fast as it comes in.

Work through it in this order

  1. Inspect the charging port with a flashlight. Lint and pocket debris build up in the port over months and physically block a full connection, even though the cable clicks in and appears to make contact. Clean gently with a wooden toothpick or a dedicated port-cleaning tool — never metal or compressed air, which can push debris deeper.
  2. Check what's running in the background while charging. A large app update, a cloud photo backup, or GPS navigation running in the background can draw more power than a standard charger provides, so the phone appears to "charge slowly" while actually just losing the race. Close background apps and check if charging speed improves.
  3. Test with a different cable, and separately, a different charger. Cables fail more often than chargers, especially right at the connector. Swap just the cable first, keeping the same charger, then swap just the charger, keeping the same cable — this isolates which part actually failed.
  4. Check your phone's battery health setting. iPhone: Settings > Battery > Battery Health. Android: varies by brand, often under Settings > Battery. If the maximum capacity has dropped significantly (well below 85%), the battery itself has degraded and charging slowdown is an expected symptom, not a fixable fault.
  5. Rule out a recent software update changing charging behavior. Some OS updates introduce more conservative charging curves specifically to protect long-term battery health, which can feel like "suddenly slower" even though nothing is actually broken. Check if the slowdown started right after your last update.
most common fix

A blocked port (step 1) is the single most common cause of a genuinely sudden slowdown — a small amount of lint reduces the electrical contact area far more than people expect.
