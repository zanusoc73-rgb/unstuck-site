Netflix keeps buffering, even though your speed test looks fine

Applies to Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, and most streaming apps, on any device.

A speed test measures the connection between you and a nearby test server for a few seconds — it says nothing about the sustained connection between you and Netflix's specific servers over the next two hours, which is a completely different path with different congestion points.

Work through it in this order

  1. Lower the streaming quality manually. In Netflix's account settings under "Playback settings," set video quality to "Medium" instead of "Auto" or "High." If buffering stops, the issue is a sustained bandwidth shortfall, not a fault — your connection can't hold 4K/HD steadily even if a quick speed test looks good.
  2. Move closer to the router or switch to a wired connection. Wi-Fi signal strength fluctuates over the course of a long video in a way a 10-second speed test won't reveal. If it's a smart TV or streaming box, an ethernet cable to the router eliminates this variable entirely.
  3. Check how many devices are active on the network right now. A phone backing up photos, a game console downloading an update, or another TV streaming in a different room all compete for the same bandwidth in real time — well after your speed test finished.
  4. Restart the streaming app or device, not just the show. Streaming apps cache a connection to a specific server, and that server can degrade mid-session. Fully close the app (or unplug a streaming stick for 10 seconds) rather than just pressing back and replaying.
  5. Test at a different time of day. If it only happens in the evening, it's very likely your internet provider's network being congested in your area during peak hours — a router or device change won't fix this; it needs a plan upgrade or a conversation with your provider.
most common fix

Switching from Wi-Fi to a wired ethernet connection for the streaming device (step 2) resolves this more often than any app setting, because Wi-Fi congestion is invisible to a one-off speed test.
