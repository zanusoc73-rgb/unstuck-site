An email is stuck in the outbox and won't send

Applies to Outlook, Apple Mail, and Gmail's app, on desktop and mobile.

A stuck outbox email is almost always one specific message with a problem — usually a large attachment or an invalid recipient address — sitting at the front of the queue and blocking every email behind it from sending, even ones with no issue at all.

Work through it in this order

  1. Open the stuck email and check the attachment size. Most providers cap outgoing attachments around 20–25MB. If the file is larger, the send will silently retry and fail forever rather than showing a clear error. Remove the attachment and use a file-sharing link (Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer) instead.
  2. Check every recipient address for typos. A single malformed address (a stray space, a missing "@", an autocomplete error) can stall the entire send with no visible warning in some mail apps. Delete and retype each address manually.
  3. Switch the account to airplane mode and back, or toggle Wi-Fi. On mobile, this forces the mail app to drop and re-establish its server connection, which clears a send that silently failed without updating its status.
  4. Delete the email from the outbox and rewrite it as a new message. If it still won't send after checking the attachment and addresses, the message itself may be corrupted in the outbox's local queue. Copy the text elsewhere, delete the stuck draft entirely, and start a fresh email.
  5. Restart the mail app fully — or, on desktop, restart your computer. A stuck outbox can be a symptom of the mail app's background sync process being frozen, which a simple app close (without fully quitting the background process) doesn't clear.
most common fix

An oversized attachment (step 1) causes this more than any other single reason — the send silently retries in the background without ever telling you why it's failing.
