Why this site exists

Most searches for a specific, annoying problem — "printer offline," "wifi connected no internet" — lead to the same dead ends: outdated forum threads, video guides that bury the answer at minute six, or pages padded with filler to rank in Google. The actual fix is usually three sentences long, and it's the three sentences nobody bothers to write clearly.

Unstuck is a small, growing library of exactly those three sentences, written out properly, in the order that actually works.

How a guide gets written

Every fix here is checked against how the problem actually behaves, not copied from another site. Guides start with the most common cause and the fastest fix, then work down to rarer causes — so most readers can stop after the first step or two.

Who's behind it

Unstuck is run independently, not by a software company trying to sell you something. It's supported by the small ads you'll see on guide pages — there's no paywall and no account required.
